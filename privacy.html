Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

SubZero is operated by Diwash Rai in Australia. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app and these legal webpages. For privacy questions or requests, contact flutterbytes2025@gmail.com.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: your Firebase user identifier and the name, email address and sign-in information supplied by Apple or Google. Apple may provide a private relay email address. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Subscription records: information you enter, such as service names, amounts, currencies, billing cycles, billing dates, categories and cancellation links, together with record timestamps.
  • Reminder information: your time zone, reminder preferences, notification history, read status and device notification tokens or installation identifiers needed to send notifications.
  • Purchase information: your app user identifier, purchased products, transaction and receipt information, purchase dates and Pro status. App stores handle payment details; SubZero does not collect your full payment-card details.
  • Support information: your email address and information you choose to send when contacting us.
  • Service information: providers may process IP addresses, technical connection information and operational logs needed to deliver and secure their services.

2. Why we use information

We use this information to sign you in, save and synchronise your records, calculate spending summaries, send reminders, verify and restore purchases, transfer Pro access when applicable, answer support requests and maintain service security. RevenueCat also processes purchase history for purchase reporting and its dashboard features.

SubZero has no advertisements and does not use Firebase Analytics. This does not mean the app collects no data: account, subscription, notification and purchase information are needed for its features.

3. Service providers and sharing

Information is processed by providers as needed for the features they supply:

  • Google Firebase: authentication, cloud data storage, backend processing and push notifications. See Firebase's privacy and security information.
  • Apple and Google: supported sign-in services, app-store transactions and notification infrastructure. See Apple's privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: purchase verification, entitlement management, restoration, transfers and purchase reporting. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gmail: support correspondence sent to our support email address.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting of these webpages. GitHub may process visitor connection information, including IP addresses. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

We may also disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to investigate misuse or protect users and the service. These webpages do not include advertising or analytics scripts.

4. Storage and international processing

App information is stored using cloud services, including Firebase. Our providers may process information outside Australia, including in the United States and other countries where they operate. Their privacy and security information explains their practices. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

5. Retention and account deletion

We retain account and subscription information while needed to provide your account and its features. You can use Delete Account in the app to delete your account and associated subscription and notification records. If deletion fails or you need assistance, email us.

Deletion of app records does not automatically erase every related record held by an app store or another provider. Limited transaction, security, support or legally required records may remain where necessary for purchase restoration, fraud prevention, dispute handling or legal obligations. Backup copies and provider logs may remain until their applicable retention cycles expire. Contact us to request deletion of other personal information we hold and an explanation of any information that must be retained.

Deleting the app from your phone is not the same as deleting your account. Transferring Pro to another account does not move your saved subscription records.

6. Your choices and requests

You can edit or remove subscription records, change reminder preferences, turn off notifications in your device settings and delete your account in the app. Lock-screen notifications may reveal a service name or billing information to someone viewing your device; you can restrict notification previews in device settings.

You can email us to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, or to raise a privacy complaint. Depending on the law that applies, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, request a portable copy or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You may raise a complaint with your applicable privacy regulator.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, processing needed to deliver requested app features is based on providing that service; security and necessary service administration rely on legitimate interests where permitted; legal recordkeeping relies on legal obligations; and optional consent-based processing can be withdrawn.

7. Children

SubZero is a general-purpose subscription tracker, not a service designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorisation, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. We will update the date and give appropriate notice of material changes through the app or another suitable channel.
